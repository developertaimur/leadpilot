LeadPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

LeadPilot ("we", "our") is a lead management tool that helps businesses communicate with their customers over WhatsApp. This page explains what information we collect and how it is used.

Information we collect

When a business uses LeadPilot, we may collect: contact names and phone numbers provided by the business (e.g. via a spreadsheet import or manual entry), the content of WhatsApp messages exchanged between the business and its customers, and basic account/configuration information needed to operate the service.

How we use this information

Information is used solely to operate the messaging service on behalf of the business using LeadPilot: sending and receiving WhatsApp messages, organizing leads and conversations, and providing the business with a record of its own customer communications.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share customer data with third parties, other than the service providers strictly necessary to operate LeadPilot (e.g. Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, our hosting and database providers).

Data retention

Data is retained for as long as the business account remains active, or as required to provide the service.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the business operating this LeadPilot instance.
